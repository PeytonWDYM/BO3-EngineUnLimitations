Failure matrix, written before implementation

1. Wrong game/helper/gate identity or changed runtime unwind metadata: refuse before publication; preserve exact PID/FILETIME and refusal stage.
2. Extra primary worker, second job member, debugger, nonzero/pending freeze result or expired absolute 30-second gate budget: refuse and contain owned child. Reuse reviewed job-freeze proof; no thread widening.
3. Any of 82 guards/19 stock count instructions, VM/migration storage, seven zero binding records or 33 stock spans differs: refuse before writes and free uncommitted relay under freeze.
4. The complete 42 plan does not partition into exactly the seven known binding records, two exact relay blocks and 33 independently pinned game originals: refuse. No arbitrary edit selection.
5. Publication/readback or final stock proof fails: restore all attempted selected edits and free relay while still frozen. Failed restoration must kill the owned child without thaw/release.
6. Owned after-apply refusal: independently observe all nine replacement readbacks and all 33 stock originals, then verify rollback before child containment.
7. Thaw or gate-release failure after commit: kill owned child; never free committed relay or roll back after thaw.
8. Natural early exit retains its raw exit code without another termination. Successful released child keeps relay mapped until exit; fixed 120-second diagnostic deadline kills only the owned job.
9. Receipt collision/optional persistence failure cannot publish outside the secure private directory or change transaction authority. Receipt uses a distinct method/suffix, stock capacity and expandedPoolEnrollment=false.
10. Production imports/source must omit debugger attachment/events/detach, SetThreadContext, debug-register writes and PSS. Detours suspended import preparation and the nine explicitly authorized transaction writes are recorded separately.
11. Abrupt owned parent death after one selected uncommitted write under the complete admission/freeze must kill the retained exact child without returning through its gate. Reuse the reviewed ParentDeath helper for this one case; no broad freeze-suite replay.

Owned targets contain inert mapped code only; parent seeding and serialized-loader setup are fixture preparation. No BlackOps3-named stand-in or actual game launch is part of this suite. Existing reviewed freeze, identity and secure-directory evidence is reused rather than replayed as a new suite.
