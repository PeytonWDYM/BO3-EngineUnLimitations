Job startup enrollment failures (written before implementation)

Scope: exact-process receipt selection and read-only VM admission on owned targets.
No game, Steam, UI, deployment, or production startup transaction.

1. Missing exact FILETIME-PID-job.json preserves stock capacity. Stale names do not enroll.
2. Any two legacy, late, or job files for one exact process refuse before runtime reads.
   Invalid content cannot fall back to a different route or stock enrollment.
3. Require exact schema1, candidate0.1.0-test.3, startupMethod late-crt-job-freeze,
   PID/FILETIME/imageBase/helperBase/gateBase/generation1, totals500001/65000,
   roots18/8, migration bytes33554432, and exactly42 edits.
4. Require jobOwned, jobMembershipVerified, primaryOnlyAdmitted, frozenForTransaction,
   thawed, committed, released, and debuggerAbsent true. Require attached, detached,
   terminated, rollbackCompleted, and liveAllocationValidated false.
5. Freeze/thaw statuses and debugRegisterWrites are integer0. threadsObserved is
   integer1. primaryThreadId is a positive bounded DWORD. Reject bool-versus-int,
   missing fields, refusal phases, malformed JSON and oversized receipts.
6. Stock diagnostic/passive methods never authorize an expanded read.
7. Real BO3 requires the fixed helper09b947... and exact capacity inventory.
   Existing fixture-only exceptions must remain restricted to non-BO3 targets.
8. A receipt does not prove live allocation. Verify all19 full count instructions,
   exact loader-ready Boot24, StateBindings48 and callback bounds twice.
9. Stock counts, damaged counts/opcode, bad Boot, wrong helper hash/base, changed
   state bindings/callbacks or unreadable code refuse with zero expanded reads.
10. Receipt content or route changes during enrollment/reverification refuse.
    A second route appearing is ambiguous even when its content is malformed.
11. Reused identity, different process handle, changed profile or exited process
    cannot reuse enrollment authority. Serialized receipts cannot grant it.
12. Preserve selected legacy and late positive enrollment, code normalization,
    stock client capacity, and ordinary stock profile behavior.
13. Retain native fixture/helper hashes, source snapshots, exact process identity,
    all read sizes, real sampled pool counts and refusal reasons as private artifacts.

The receipt fixture tests enrollment metadata. It does not claim a job-freeze
startup transaction or real-game allocation. Native transaction proof is separate.
